# Observability Checklist: Cross-Provider Cost Telemetry
**Created**: 2026-06-09 | **Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md)

Requirements-quality ("unit tests for English") review of the Observability/telemetry surface of E007 — OTel GenAI emission, semconv pinning, collector normalization, telemetry/tool-span parity, diagnostic retention, and unpriced-sample visibility. Each item interrogates whether the requirements are clear, complete, consistent, measurable, and traceable — not whether code behaves.
