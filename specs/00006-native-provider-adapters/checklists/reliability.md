# Checklist: Reliability (Requirements Quality)

**Created**: 2026-06-09 | **Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md)

Unit Tests for English — does the spec/plan define the reliability requirements precisely, completely, and consistently enough to test, including every failure mode and its expected recovery behavior? Scope: E006 Reliability domain (retry/backoff classification, breaker separation, stream-interruption, malformed-JSON, runaway-loop bounds, missing-key guard, graceful degradation).
